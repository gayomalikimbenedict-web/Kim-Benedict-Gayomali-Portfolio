Sechurplets CRM

Built, tested and ready to run.

  • Careful sign-inServer-side sessions, CSRF protection and a limit of five wrong tries per 15 minutes.
  • Bulk changes, all or nothingUp to 1,000 matching leads change in one transaction, after the server re-counts them.
  • Audit trail without personal dataAction, lead number and status change are recorded. Names and contact details are not.
  • CSV in and outImports are all or nothing. Exports defend against spreadsheet formula injection.
  • Consent trackingThe server stamps the consent date. Old audit entries can be purged on a retention schedule.
  • Ready to runFlask and SQLite behind Waitress, verified backups, and 378 automated tests.
